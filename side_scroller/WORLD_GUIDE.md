# Windswept world guide

This first playable world pass contains **32 safe towns/regions and 33 hunting maps: 65 maps total**. Each new region has an original name, scenery theme, resident, hunting route, scaled enemies and equipment drops. The broader world currently shares enemy families and map-layout templates. Character artwork is unchanged; the playable Warrior and Thief progression stays intact. Pirate content is excluded.

## Travel

Press **M** to browse every region. Choose a town and its hunting route to inspect platforms, portals and directions from your current area. Browsing does not teleport you. Walk to the named portal and press **Up**, or your rebound Interact key. All routes have a return connection.

**From Willowbridge:** go left to the Gullhaven portal, cross Saltwind Road, and enter Gullhaven. Its portals lead toward the starting island, Skylume and overseas routes. Willowbridge also connects through hunting routes to Starbough, Ironridge, Shadecross, Hollowroot and Farmeadow. Whisperwood and Briar Hollow remain accessible through the original portal.

New towns restore health and become the death-respawn checkpoint for the current session. Restarting/loading a character still begins in Willowbridge; character stats, skills, loot and key bindings remain saved. Captain Elowen still advances both playable routes. Warden Bram in Ironridge advances Warriors; Mara Vex in Shadecross advances Thieves.

## Combat and progression

Regional hunting routes use visible enemy levels. Enemy HP, damage and XP scale with the route level; starter enemies remain weak as your character grows. Routes at level 15+ can drop Briar equipment, level 35+ Wayfarer equipment, and level 60+ Aethersteel/Aetherweave equipment. Towns contain no hostile spawns. Current level cap remains 100.

Snow, desert, city, toy, underwater, highland, temple and coastal maps have distinct terrain. Regional buildings include harbor houses, treehouses, warrior lodges, city guild houses, root inns, observatories, snow cottages, toy clocktowers, desert homes, pagodas, alchemy labs and shrines. Underwater maps currently use the same walking/double-jump controls as the rest of the prototype.

The named world and travel routes are playable. Regional quest stories, additional monster families, more bosses and additional character classes are subsequent content passes.
