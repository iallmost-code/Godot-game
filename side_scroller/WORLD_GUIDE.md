# Windswept world guide

The world contains **32 safe towns/regions, 33 regional hunting routes, 10 deeper hunting grounds and 12 boss lairs: 87 maps total**. Each new region has an original name, scenery theme, resident, hunting route, scaled enemies and equipment drops. The broader world currently shares enemy families and map-layout templates. All four classes (Warrior, Mage, Archer, Thief) and their ten job paths are playable. Pirate content is excluded.

## Travel

Press **M** to browse every region. Choose a town and its hunting route to inspect platforms, portals and directions from your current area. Browsing does not teleport you. Walk to the named portal and press **Up**, or your rebound Interact key. All routes have a return connection.

**From Willowbridge:** go left to the Gullhaven portal, cross Saltwind Road, and enter Gullhaven. Its portals lead toward the starting island, Skylume and overseas routes. Willowbridge also connects through hunting routes to Starbough, Ironridge, Shadecross, Hollowroot and Farmeadow. Whisperwood and Briar Hollow remain accessible through the original portal.

New towns restore health and become the death-respawn checkpoint for the current session. Restarting/loading a character still begins in Willowbridge; character stats, skills, loot and key bindings remain saved. Captain Elowen advances every class. Warden Bram (Ironridge) advances Warriors, Arcanist Ilyra (Starbough) advances Mages, Mara Vex (Shadecross) advances Thieves and Ranger Vale (Thornscar Lodge) advances Archers.

## Combat and progression

Each regional hunting route is populated with themed monsters at the route's level (for example snails on Snailgrass Path, toys in the Clockwork Warrens, fish and crabs in the Coral Cathedral, drakes in Wyrmwood Reach, monks in Echoes of Tomorrow). Monster HP, damage and XP scale with the route level. Routes below level 35 drop Forest/Briar gear; higher routes drop level-banded gear for every weapon type.

Ten deeper hunting grounds continue the journey to level 200, and twelve boss lairs open from their towns: Dewdrop Meadow (Farmeadow), Mossvale Thicket (Starbough), Rotfen Marsh, Blight Mother Cap and Lesser Hellwing (Hollowroot), Emberpeak Canyon and Elder Stumpus (Ironridge), Gelatinous Monarch (Shadecross), Grand Shellmaw (Sunpetal Cove), Mother Cap (Moonpetal Shrine), Frostspire Peaks, Bonepit Mine and Molten Colossus (Frostmere), Clockwork Spire and Chronovore (Tickletop), Tidegrave Depths and Abyssal Leviathan (Tideglass), Skyreach Plateau and Thornscale Wyrm (Wyrmrest), Crimson Hellwing (Thornscar Lodge), Timeless Sanctum and Gloam Idol (Hourglass Sanctum). Level cap is 200.

Snow, desert, city, toy, underwater, highland, temple and coastal maps have distinct terrain. Regional buildings include harbor houses, treehouses, warrior lodges, city guild houses, root inns, observatories, snow cottages, toy clocktowers, desert homes, pagodas, alchemy labs and shrines. Underwater maps currently use the same walking/double-jump controls as the rest of the game.

Regional quest stories and hand-drawn sprites for the new monsters are subsequent content passes.
